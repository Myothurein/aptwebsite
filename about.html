WHO WE ARE

About Us

APT (Aung Paing Tun Company Limited) was established in 2015, with K.T.Z Company as its associate corporate partner responsible for distribution. We focus on providing premium quality pharmaceutical & healthcare products towards Myanmar patients and consumers.

Our products range from Poland, Cyprus, Germany, and other suppliers including US-FDA and EU-GMP approved plants. We have a diverse product portfolio specializing in the antibiotics and injection market.

Starting from January 2023, APT established a separate Marketing team to promote food supplements and consumer products.

Quality & Reach

  • Global Sourcing: Imported from Poland, Germany, Cyprus & International Suppliers.
  • Certified Standards: US-FDA and EU-GMP Approved Facilities.
  • Market Expertise: Specialized in Antibiotics, Injections, Supplements & Consumer Healthcare.

Managing Director's Message

Aung Paing Tun was established by the Managing Director, Mr. Than Aung, the co-founder & acting General Manager of K.T.Z pharmaceutical distribution company founded since 1994. To extend the business, Mr. Than founded Aung Paing Tun Company in 2015 to offer a different variety of products towards Myanmar consumers and serve new markets with creative and innovative methods.
